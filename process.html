The Process

Five acts, from
conversation to cloth.

A commission unfolds slowly and deliberately. Each stage is private, unhurried, and held at the Atlanta atelier — one client at a time.

01

The Consultation

We begin with a private conversation over coffee or whisky — at the studio or by appointment near you. We explore the occasion, your wardrobe, the way you live and move, and the story the suit must tell. Nothing is measured yet; first, we understand.

02

The Cloth

From over a thousand fabrics — worsteds, flannels, silks and stretch wools from heritage English and Italian mills — we choose the cloth, the weight, the lining, and the details: horn or metal buttons, the lapel, the pockets, a monogram spoken only to you.

03

The Measure

Thirty-plus precise measurements, and more than numbers — posture, stance, the fall of a shoulder, the way you carry yourself. It becomes a body profile, kept for every commission that follows, so the second suit begins where the first left off.

04

The Fittings

A muslin fitting first — the suit in rehearsal — then the refinements. We adjust and re-adjust across two fittings and micro-adjustments, until the balance is exact and the cloth learns your body rather than the other way around.

05

The Reveal & Beyond

The final fitting, and the suit delivered in a premium NOIR garment bag. For those who ask, the concierge continues — at-home fittings, a wardrobe roadmap, and, for the wedding, coordination of the day itself down to the smallest thread.
